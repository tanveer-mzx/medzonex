What Is Pharmacy Inventory Management Software?

Pharmacy inventory management software helps medical stores organise medicine stock and maintain important inventory information in a digital system.

It supports stock management activities such as monitoring medicine quantities, maintaining batch details and tracking expiry information.

Why Is Medicine Stock Management Important?

Medicine inventory is an important part of running a pharmacy. Medical stores need visibility into available stock, batch information and expiry dates to manage their inventory.

Low-stock awareness also helps pharmacy owners identify medicines that may need replenishment.
